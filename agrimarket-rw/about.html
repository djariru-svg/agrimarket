Ikibazo dukemura

Abahinzi benshi mu Rwanda bagurisha umusaruro wabo ku giciro gito cyane kuko nta makuru bafite ku isoko, kandi abaguzi nabo bakabura aho bagura umusaruro uva ku bahinzi b'ibanze ku giciro cyiza.

AgriMarket RW ni platform ihuje abahinzi n'abaguzi mu buryo bworoshye, bwizewe kandi bworoshye. Umuhinzi ashobora gushyira umusaruro we, umuguzi akabona aho agura hafi ye.

Innovation yacu

• Matching system ihuje abahinzi n'abaguzi hashingiwe ku hantu n'igiciro
• Mobile Money integration
• Ratings & Reviews system
• Farmer Dashboard yo gucunga umusaruro
• Location-based product discovery
